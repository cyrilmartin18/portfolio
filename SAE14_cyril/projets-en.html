Academic Projects

SAÉ 11: Choosing a Password Manager for a Small Business

BUT Networks and Telecommunications, IUT of Rouen Normandy · (Month and year) · Pair project, 12 hours of independent work

Context

In this business scenario, I took on the role of Information Systems Security Officer (ISSO) for a small business with 15 employees that was moving to new premises. The manager, who had limited technical knowledge, wanted to require employees to use a password manager. Storing passwords in web browsers was not allowed.

Work Completed

I compared several password managers according to their storage methods (local or cloud-based), security, dependence on third-party companies, cost and interoperability. I then selected (name of the password manager). I prepared two documents: a report for the manager, including a comparison table and an analysis of risks related to user behaviour, and an illustrated tutorial with screenshots to help employees learn how to use the tool. My role in the pair project included: research, writing, screenshots and testing.

What I Learned

This project taught me how to compare different solutions using clear arguments, explain technical topics to non-specialists, write clear documentation and work as part of a team. (Add what you found difficult or what you would do differently next time.)
